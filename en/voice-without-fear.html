Voice Without Fear

For people who think “I hate my voice”, feel afraid to sing in front of others, or freeze when they try to improvise.

See the program

If hearing your own voice feels uncomfortable

The program starts with short experiments rather than demanding a “good” or “correct” sound.

If you are afraid to sing

You explore pitch, timbre, volume, rhythm and unusual sounds without turning every attempt into a performance.

Not vocal technique

This is a creative practice focused on experimentation, improvisation and reducing constant self-evaluation. It is not psychotherapy and does not replace professional mental-health care.
