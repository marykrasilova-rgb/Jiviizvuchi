Free creative self-reflection tool

Notice. Create. Notice again. Distinguish. Choose.

Instead of consuming another piece of content, give your current state a form — with your voice, movement, drawing or words.

Try it free

Four ways to express

Record a sound or improvisation, move, draw, or write. You do not need to sing, dance or draw “well”.

Built for reflection

Rate what you feel before and after, notice what changed, separate your own response from automatic expectations, and choose a small next step.

Not therapy, not diagnosis

The journal is an additional self-observation tool. It does not diagnose or treat mental-health conditions and does not replace professional care.
