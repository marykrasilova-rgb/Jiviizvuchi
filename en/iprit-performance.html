Theatre · music · poetry · 2016–2018

Yperite

A solo performance in poetry and music created by director-performer Sergey Chernykh with composer, musician and Playback Theatre musician Maria Krasilova.

At a glance

The performance premiered at Akadempark in Novosibirsk Akademgorodok in December 2016. It explored the cultural rupture of World War I through Russian and European poetry, early twentieth-century music and live stage composition.

Maria Krasilova’s role

Maria was the musical performer and music editor. She shaped a historical sound world around 1914–1918, bringing works by Debussy, Schoenberg, Bartók, Prokofiev, Webern, Vertinsky and others into dialogue with poetry by Blok, Mayakovsky, Rilke, Gumilev, Owen, Kharms and Vvedensky.

For the festival version in Moldova, the ending included Maria’s original composition for voice and a plastic bag. Maria recalls that it was described during the festival discussion as “a requiem for war” — a listener’s response, not the work’s official title.

Premiere and recognition

  • December 2016, Novosibirsk: premiere at Akadempark.
  • December 2017, Chișinău: laureate of the international chamber theatre festival Moldfest.Rampa.Ru.
  • February 2018, Novosibirsk: special jury prize and diploma at the Russian chamber and solo-performance festival One. Two. Three.
  • July 2018, Moscow: performance at the Marina Tsvetaeva House Museum.

Historical context

The artistic material refers specifically to World War I and the years 1914–1918. The project predates the war that began in 2022; its subject is the way war breaks a cultural order and changes language, listening and human presence.

Connection to Maria’s current work

The experience continues in Maria’s practice: listening to a story, finding its musical form, and connecting voice, object sound, text and improvisation without taking meaning away from the participant.
